TITLE:
L2-Sized Cooperative GPU-Accelerated Primal-Shortened ISD: Recovering TII-237 (mt = 135) on Commodity GPUs

AUTHORS:
Raullen Chai (MachineFi Labs)

CONTACT EMAIL:
raullen@machinefi.com

CATEGORY:
Public-Key Cryptography

KEYWORDS:
McEliece cryptosystem, binary Goppa codes, information-set decoding, GPU cryptanalysis, TII McEliece Challenges

ABSTRACT (plain text, ready for web form):
Structural key recovery on binary Goppa codes has progressed rapidly during 2026 on the public TII McEliece Track-2 challenges. Most recently, MiniMax introduced Primal-Shortened Information-Set Decoding (ISD) (Route A) alongside HOVER (Route B), recovering 94 Track-2 keys and reaching codimension mt = 126 (TII-228, 2,463 physical CPU core-hours in 57.1 min on 2,048-5,120 EPYC cores) and mt = 120 (TII-250, 66,722 core-hours on 5,120-10,240 cores). Building directly on MiniMax's Route-A algebraic recovery framework, Mode-6 Stern-style sampler (p = 3 + 3, l = 17), and shortening schedule, we redesign the minimum odd-weight codeword harvester for GPU memory hierarchies. Combining (i) lossless 16-byte ulonglong2 syndrome packing without left-index storage (mt <= 145), (ii) cooperative execution over G = 4 concurrent hash tables (35.7 MB total footprint, sized to fit nominal L4/A100 L2 caches), and (iii) full-SM batched random systematic reduction eliminating a 74.0% Gaussian-elimination bottleneck, we sustain 9,462 it/s on TII-237 and 11,035 it/s on TII-245 on a single NVIDIA L4 GPU. On the benchmark instance TII-245 (mt = 108), a single L4 harvests 24,500 codewords in 1,175.5 s (1,185.3 s end-to-end including 9.78 s SageMath algebraic lift), compared to 2,500 s harvesting (26,352 words; 2,510 s end-to-end) on a 64-core Xeon Gold 6338 server (2.12x end-to-end, ~1.9x target-normalized). Using commodity Colab GPUs (~22.1 aggregate GPU-hours, ~7.0e8 ISD iterations, 68.97 total account CU across development and production), we harvest 29,742 verified weight-31 codewords and recover a verified equivalent private key for TII-237 (n = 500, m = 9, t = 15, mt = 135) in 23.23 s of algebraic post-processing—to our knowledge, as of November 22, 2026, the highest codimension mt among publicly documented Track-2 solutions. These experiments do not demonstrate practical key recovery against proposed Classic McEliece parameter sets (mt >= 768).
