TITLE:
Packing Stern into L2: GPU-Accelerated Primal-Shortened ISD for High-Codimension Binary Goppa Key Recovery

AUTHORS:
Raullen Chai (MachineFi Labs)
Xinxin Fan (MachineFi Labs)

CONTACT EMAIL:
raullen@machinefi.com, xinxin@machinefi.com

CATEGORY:
Public-Key Cryptography

KEYWORDS:
McEliece cryptosystem, binary Goppa codes, information-set decoding, GPU cryptanalysis, TII McEliece Challenges

ABSTRACT (plain text, ready for web form):
Primal-Shortened Information-Set Decoding (ISD) with quadratic polar lifting recently pushed TII McEliece Track-2 key recovery to mt = 126 (TII-228, 2,463 CPU core-hours), with over 99% of runtime spent harvesting weight-(2t+1) codewords. Porting MiniMax's Mode-6 Stern sampler directly to one block per SM leaves 1.0-1.5 GB of hash tables active and stalls on global memory. For mt <= 145, we pack all mt - 17 <= 128 remaining syndrome bits into one 16-byte ulonglong2 without left-triple indices, share G in {4, 8} cooperative tables inside GPU L2 cache, batch 64G random systematic reductions per launch, and widen the shortening prefix n' to cut modeled GPU-hours by 34%-47%.

Across four Track-2 challenges (mt = 108 to 144), every recovered private key passes both the official SageMath checker and a standalone pure-Python verifier. Eight Modal L40S GPUs solve TII-228 (mt = 126) in 18.9 min (2.15 GPU-hours, 3.0x faster in wall-clock than MiniMax's EPYC cluster). We further recover keys for the previously undocumented challenges TII-237 (mt = 135, 22.1 L4 GPU-hours) and TII-235 (mt = 144, 4.30 h across ten L40S GPUs, 39.62 GPU-hours, 17.97 s lift), using 127 of 128 packed bits. These results do not demonstrate practical recovery against Classic McEliece parameters (mt >= 768).
